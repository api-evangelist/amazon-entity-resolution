AWS Brazil now provides an automated distribution workflow through the AWS Brazil 2P Distribution Program. Eligible non-Brazilian independent software vendors (ISVs) grant AWS Brazil the right to distribute their Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) product licenses, and AWS Brazil distributes them to customers in Brazil. AWS Brazil is the seller of record, invoicing Brazilian customers in Brazilian Reais (BRL) with applicable Brazilian taxes.
