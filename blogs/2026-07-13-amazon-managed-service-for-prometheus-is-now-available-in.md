Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus is now available in Asia Pacific (New Zealand) Region. Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus is a fully managed, Prometheus-compatible monitoring service that makes it easy to monitor and alert on operational metrics at scale. Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus is available in multiple AWS Regions .
