Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus collector, a fully-managed agentless collector for Prometheus metrics, is now available in Asia Pacific (New Zealand). Customers in New Zealand can now run their Prometheus-compatible monitoring workloads in-region, with lower latency for local workloads and the ability to keep their data in-country. Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus collector discovers and collects Prometheus metrics from your container workloads without the need to run and operate collection agents yourself.
