AWS Marketplace now supports auto-renewals for private offers. This capability helps sellers and buyers maintain active software and service subscriptions without re-negotiation or re-purchasing at the end of the term. Negotiated pricing and contract terms carry forward automatically when buyers and sellers agree to renew.
