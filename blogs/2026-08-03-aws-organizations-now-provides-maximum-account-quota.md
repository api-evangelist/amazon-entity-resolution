AWS Organizations customers can now view their maximum number of accounts quota and its utilization directly through AWS Service Quotas instead of relying on AWS Support or AWS account teams to determine their current account limit. Customers can now proactively plan account growth with ease by monitoring their current account quota utilization and requesting increases before reaching their limit. They can view this quota by logging into management account and accessing the Service Quotas console or calling the Service Quotas GetServiceQuota API.
