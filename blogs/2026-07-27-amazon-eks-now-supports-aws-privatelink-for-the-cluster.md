Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (Amazon EKS) now supports AWS PrivateLink for the cluster OIDC discovery and JWKS endpoint . You can now reach the endpoint used by IAM roles for service accounts (IRSA) privately from your VPC without requiring internet egress. Each EKS cluster publishes public signing keys at its OIDC endpoint for IRSA.
