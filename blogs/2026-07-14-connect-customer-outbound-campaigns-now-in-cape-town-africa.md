Connect Customer Outbound Campaigns digital channels (SMS, WhatsApp, and email) are now available in the Africa (Cape Town) Region. Businesses in the Region can now use Connect Customer's proactive outreach capabilities to create targeted, personalized engagement campaigns for service updates, promotional offers, appointment reminders, and product usage tips. Organizations configure and manage these campaigns through the Connect Customer admin website and Outbound Campaigns APIs.
