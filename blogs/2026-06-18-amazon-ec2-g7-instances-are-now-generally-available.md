AWS announces general availability of Amazon EC2 G7 instances, accelerated by NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs, delivering up to 4.6x AI inference and up to 2.1x graphics performance over G6 instances. G7 instances feature up to eight GPUs with 32GB memory each, custom Intel Xeon 6 processors, and up to 700 Gbps EFA networking bandwidth.
