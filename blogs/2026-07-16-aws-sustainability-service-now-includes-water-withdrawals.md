Customers can now view annual water withdrawals data associated with their AWS workloads in AWS Sustainability, alongside existing carbon emissions data. This enhancement helps organizations gain comprehensive visibility into their environmental impact across carbon and water. Water withdrawals data is available by AWS Region, service, and AWS account on an annual basis through the AWS Sustainability console and API.
