Now generally available, Amazon Quick lets you build custom applications by simply describing them in natural language. Create project trackers, customer dashboards, and training portals in minutes instead of months. Whether you're a product manager, finance lead, HR partner, or ops analyst, you can now turn ideas into fully functional apps without writing any code.
