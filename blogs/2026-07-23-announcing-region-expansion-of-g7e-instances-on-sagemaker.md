G7e instances feature up to 8 NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs with 96 GB of memory per GPU, 5th Generation Intel Xeon processors, and up to 1,600 Gbps of Elastic Fabric Adapter networking bandwidth, delivering up to 2.3x inference performance compared to previous-generation G6e instances.
