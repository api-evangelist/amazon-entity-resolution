Amazon Bedrock now supports SpaceXAI Grok 4.6, a frontier model built for coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work, with US Geo and Global cross-Region inference. Customers can now access Grok 4.6 at scale with cross-Region inference routing requests across multiple AWS Regions for higher throughput and lower inference costs. Cross-Region inference automatically routes inference requests across multiple AWS Regions to give you higher throughput, without you needing to manage capacity across multiple Regions.
