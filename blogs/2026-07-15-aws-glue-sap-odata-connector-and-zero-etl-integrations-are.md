AWS Glue SAP OData connector and zero-ETL integrations are now available in AWS GovCloud (US-West) and AWS GovCloud (US-East) Regions, with support for Amazon DynamoDB, Salesforce, and SAP OData as sources. Customers operating in regulated environments can now replicate data from these sources into Amazon Redshift, Amazon S3, or other supported destinations without building or maintaining custom data pipelines. The SAP OData connector allows you to extract data from SAP systems exposing OData services, eliminating the need for custom extraction logic or third-party middleware.
