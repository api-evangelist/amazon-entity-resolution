Two new Anthropic models are now available in the Kiro IDE and CLI for the AWS GovCloud (US) Regions. Claude Opus 5.5 is Anthropic's most capable Opus model yet and a step up from Opus 5 on long-running agentic coding work. Opus 5.5 thinks adaptively on every request, deciding how much effort each task needs, and completes tasks with roughly 40% fewer tool calls and about half the tokens of Opus 5 in Kiro's internal benchmarking.
