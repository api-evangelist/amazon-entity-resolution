AWS Transfer Family now lets you choose a custom log group in Amazon CloudWatch Logs for managed workflow execution logs. You can organize workflow logs to monitor individual workflows separately or bring logs from related workflows together. Previously, every workflow attached to a Transfer Family server sent its execution logs to that server’s CloudWatch log group, so you could not set a separate log destination for each workflow.
