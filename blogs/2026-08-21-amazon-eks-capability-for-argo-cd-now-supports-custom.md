The Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (Amazon EKS) Capability for Argo CD now supports custom configuration through a standard argocd-cm ConfigMap in your cluster. This capability gives you a fully managed GitOps continuous delivery experience, and you can now tune it to fit how your teams work. You can define custom health checks for your Custom Resources, customize the Argo CD UI banner content, adjust how the capability watches and compares the resources it manages, and more.
