Partner Revenue Measurement (PRM) provides measurement of AWS consumption driven by Partner solutions. Today, PRM adds multi-Partner support to Resource Tagging, so that multiple AWS Partners can receive revenue attribution for the same AWS resource. Previously, a resource carried only one Partner tag, aws-apn-id , so when multiple Partners contributed to the same workload, only one Partner received attribution.
