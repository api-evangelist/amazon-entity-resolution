Amazon S3 Metadata is now available in six additional AWS Regions: Asia Pacific (Hyderabad), Asia Pacific (Malaysia), Asia Pacific (New Zealand), Asia Pacific (Taipei), Asia Pacific (Thailand), and Mexico (Central). S3 Metadata is a fast and easy way to instantly discover and understand your S3 data with automated, easily queried metadata that updates in near real time. This helps you curate, identify, and use your S3 data for business analytics, real-time inference applications, and more.
