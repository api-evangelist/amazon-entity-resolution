Amazon Quick now integrates with Microsoft Purview to enforce data loss prevention (DLP) policies across your Quick environment. Organizations need to ensure that sensitive files aren't shared outside approved channels. With this integration, IT administrators and security teams can apply their existing Purview sensitivity labels to automatically control how files are handled in Quick capabilities such as chat, spaces, and knowledge bases.
