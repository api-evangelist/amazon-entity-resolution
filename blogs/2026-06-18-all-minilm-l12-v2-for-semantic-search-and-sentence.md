AWS announces availability of all-MiniLM-L12-v2 in Amazon SageMaker JumpStart, a Sentence Transformers model mapping sentences and paragraphs to a 384-dimensional dense vector space. It is suitable for semantic search, text clustering, sentence similarity, information retrieval, document clustering, duplicate detection, and paraphrase identification.
