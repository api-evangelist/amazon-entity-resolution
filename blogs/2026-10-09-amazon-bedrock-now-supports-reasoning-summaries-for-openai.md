Amazon Bedrock now supports the reasoning.summary parameter for OpenAI models through the Responses API. This capability lets you request a human-readable summary of a model’s reasoning alongside its response, helping you understand its approach to complex tasks such as coding, analysis, and multi-step problem solving. Reasoning summaries give developers additional context for evaluating responses, debugging applications, and showing users how a model approached a request.
