Amazon ECS service auto scaling now detects and responds to load changes faster with high resolution (20-second) metrics and metric publishing optimizations. AWS benchmarking shows scale-out triggering improved from 363 seconds to 86 seconds (76% faster) and total scaling and provisioning time improved from 386 seconds to 109 seconds (72% faster).
