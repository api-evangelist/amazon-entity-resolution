AWS Marketplace now enables India-based sellers to upload and manage their seller signatures directly through AWS Partner Central, eliminating the previous manual, email-based submission process. Sellers located in India are required to provide a valid seller signature for tax invoicing and regulatory compliance. This launch consolidates Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) registration and seller signature management into a single, centralized location purpose-built for India-based sellers transacting on AWS Marketplace.
