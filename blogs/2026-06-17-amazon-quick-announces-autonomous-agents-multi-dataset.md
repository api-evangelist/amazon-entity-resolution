AWS announces new Amazon Quick features including autonomous agents, multi-dataset analytics, and a redesigned activity feed. Autonomous agents let users describe tasks in natural language with granular autonomy levels, while multi-dataset analytics enables natural-language querying across sources including Snowflake and relational databases, inheriting semantic intelligence from catalogs like AWS Glue, Databricks Unity Catalog, and Collibra.
