Amazon CloudWatch Logs now supports intelligent storage tiering, which automatically classifies your log data across three storage tiers - Standard (existing), Infrequent Access, and Archive Instant Access based on access patterns. This allows you to store logs in Amazon CloudWatch for extended periods at lower-cost tiers without any operational overhead. With today's launch, customers can now retain high-volume verbose logs needed to be stored for longer periods at a lower cost in Amazon CloudWatch.
