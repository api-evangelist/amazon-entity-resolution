AWS Builder Center is a platform that brings together the global builder community, offering articles, workshops, sandbox environments, events, and learning resources in one place. Previously limited to desktop and web access, the experience is now available as a mobile app on iOS and Android. Builders worldwide can stay connected and engaged with the community from anywhere, using their AWS Builder ID to remain signed in across sessions.
