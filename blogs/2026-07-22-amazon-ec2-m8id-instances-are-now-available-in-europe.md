Amazon EC2 M8id instances are now available in (Ireland). These instances are powered by custom Intel Xeon 6 processors and deliver up to 43% higher performance and 3.3x more memory bandwidth compared to previous generation M6id instances. M8id instances offer up to 384 vCPUs, 1.5TiB of memory, and 22.8TB of NVMe SSD storage, 3x more than previous generation instances.
