Nested virtualization is now accessible on additional Intel platforms and Regions, expanding to C7i, R7i, M7i, C8id, R8id, M8id, C7i-flex, M7i-flex, I7i, C8i-flex, R8i-flex, M8i-flex, and X8i instances. The feature also extends to US GovCloud (US-East) and US GovCloud (US-West).
