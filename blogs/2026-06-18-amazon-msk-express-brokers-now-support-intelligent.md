Amazon MSK Provisioned clusters with Express brokers now support Intelligent Rebalancing on all existing clusters at no additional cost, previously restricted to newly created clusters. Intelligent Rebalancing maximizes capacity utilization by optimally rebalancing Kafka resources, performing operations up to 180 times faster compared to Standard brokers.
