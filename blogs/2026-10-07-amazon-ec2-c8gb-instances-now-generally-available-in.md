Starting today, Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (Amazon EC2) C8gb instances, powered by the latest-generation AWS Graviton4 processors, are available in the US East (Ohio), US West (N. California), EU (Frankfurt, Ireland, Paris, Stockholm), and Africa (Cape Town) regions. The new instances provide up to 30% better compute performance than AWS Graviton3-based EC2 instances.
