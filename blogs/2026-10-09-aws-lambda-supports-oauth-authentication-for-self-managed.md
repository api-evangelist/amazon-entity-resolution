AWS Lambda now supports OAuth authentication for self-managed Apache Kafka event source mappings (ESM), including Kafka clusters that customers run themselves and managed offerings such as Confluent Cloud, Aiven, and Redpanda. With this launch, customers can authenticate their Lambda Kafka consumers through their enterprise identity provider, helping them meet the security and compliance requirements for their Kafka applications.
