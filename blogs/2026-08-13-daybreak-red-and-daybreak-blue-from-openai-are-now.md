Security teams can now access Daybreak Red and Daybreak Blue from OpenAI on Amazon Bedrock. Both are part of Daybreak, the cyber defense initiative from OpenAI that gives defenders governed access to frontier AI for cybersecurity work. Daybreak Blue is the starting point for most security teams across defensive workflows including vulnerability discovery, detection engineering, and incident response.
