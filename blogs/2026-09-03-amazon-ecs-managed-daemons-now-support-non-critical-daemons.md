Amazon Elastic Container Service (Amazon ECS) now support non-critical Managed Daemons for ECS Managed Instances . You can configure a daemon as non-critical so that your mission-critical application tasks continue running uninterrupted, even when a daemon fails, stops, or becomes unhealthy. Amazon ECS Managed Daemons lets you centrally deploy and manage software agents independently of application deployments to ensure consistent coverage and compliance across your container infrastructure.
