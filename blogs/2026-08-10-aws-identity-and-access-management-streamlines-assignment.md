AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) launched account access manager, a feature that streamlines assignment of IAM roles to workforce users. Administrators use account access manager to assign the IAM roles in their AWS accounts to the workforce users and groups in AWS IAM Identity Center. The feature brings together permissions management flexibility, user awareness, and a single point of federation.
