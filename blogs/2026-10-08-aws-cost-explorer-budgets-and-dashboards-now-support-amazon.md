AWS Cost Explorer, AWS Budgets, and AWS Cost Management Dashboards now let you analyze Amazon Bedrock costs by product attributes, a new dimension that breaks down Bedrock cost by model, model provider, inference type, and feature. In Cost Explorer and Dashboards, you can group and filter costs by these attributes. In Budgets, you can filter budgets by the same attributes.
