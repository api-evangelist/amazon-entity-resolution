Amazon Connect Talent is now generally available, giving talent acquisition leaders an AI-powered hiring solution that accelerates candidate selection at scale. Informed by decades of Amazon's hiring science, Amazon Connect Talent uses AI agents to conduct structured voice interviews, administer evidence based assessments, and score candidates consistently, freeing recruiters to focus on strategic decisions. Candidates interview 24/7 from any device.
