Amazon WorkSpaces Applications now supports Microsoft OneDrive for Business and Google Drive as persistent storage options on multi-session fleets. Users streaming on multi-session fleets can now connect their OneDrive or Google Drive accounts and access, save, and sync their cloud-based files directly within their streaming sessions, in addition to the existing home folder backed by Amazon S3. Multi-session fleets allow multiple users to share a single fleet instance, enabling organizations to achieve cost efficiencies by increasing session density across their infrastructure.
