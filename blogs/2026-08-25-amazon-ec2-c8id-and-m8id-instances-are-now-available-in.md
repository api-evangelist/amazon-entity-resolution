Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (Amazon EC2) C8id and M8id instances with up to 22.8 TB of local NVMe-based SSD block-level storage are now available in additional regions. C8id instances are now available in Asia Pacific (Sydney) and Canada (Central) and M8id instances are available in Asia Pacific (Mumbai) and Canada (Central). Amazon EC2 C8id and M8id instances offer 3 times more vCPUs, memory and local storage compared to previous sixth-generation instances.
