You can now use container images from registries such as Seqera (Wave), NVIDIA NGC, and Google Artifact Registry with AWS HealthOmics expanded support for third-party container registries. You can use the containerized tools your workflows already rely on, without manually migrating images into Amazon ECR or editing your workflow definitions. AWS HealthOmics is a HIPAA-eligible service that helps healthcare and life sciences customers accelerate scientific breakthroughs at scale with fully managed bioinformatics workflows.
