Amazon S3, including S3 Tables and S3 Vectors, is now available on the Vercel Marketplace and v0 by Vercel. Developers can create and connect S3 resources in seconds without leaving their development environment, getting the durability, scalability, and performance of S3 through Vercel's streamlined provisioning workflow. Millions of customers rely on S3 for low-cost, high-performance storage to power web and AI applications, but until now provisioning it from Vercel required navigating to the AWS Management Console.
