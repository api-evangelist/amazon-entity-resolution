GPT-5.6 Terra and Luna are now generally available on Amazon Bedrock in AWS GovCloud (US-West) and AWS GovCloud (US-East), bringing the smartest family of models from OpenAI yet to Bedrock's next-generation inference engine built for high-performance, security and reliability. GPT-5.6 sets a new standard for intelligence and efficiency, allowing you to solve harder problems in less time and with more intelligence per token. The two models span capability tiers from balanced performance (Terra) to fast, cost-efficient inference (Luna).
