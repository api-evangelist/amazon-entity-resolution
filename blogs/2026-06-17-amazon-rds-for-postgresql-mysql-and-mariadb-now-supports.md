AWS Graviton5-based M9g database instances are now generally available for Amazon RDS supporting PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MariaDB, providing up to 30% performance improvement and up to 23% price/performance enhancement over Graviton4-based instances. M9g DB instances feature new 24xlarge and 48xlarge sizes with up to 192 vCPU, up to 100 Gbps networking, and up to 72 Gbps EBS bandwidth.
