AWS Elemental MediaPackage now offers Dynamic Multiview, a server-side capability that composes multiple live video sources into viewer-selected tiled layouts on demand. Content providers can deliver multi-angle, multi-game, and personalized viewing experiences as standard HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) and DASH (Dynamic Adaptive Streaming over HTTP) streams playable on most modern consumer devices, televisions, and set top boxes without custom player development. Dynamic Multiview operates entirely in the compressed domain, combining individually encoded sources without re-encoding or compositing.
