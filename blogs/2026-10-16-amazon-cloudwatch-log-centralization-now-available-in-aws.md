Amazon CloudWatch log centralization is now available in AWS GovCloud (US) and 13 additional commercial Regions. CloudWatch log centralization copies log data from multiple AWS accounts and Regions into a single destination account, integrating with AWS Organizations for efficient aggregation without the need for custom solutions. With this launch, organizations in the AWS GovCloud (US) partition and commercial partition can each centralize logs across their accounts and Regions for centralized monitoring.
