The AWS Transfer Family console now supports SVG files for logos and ICO files for favicons in web apps, with upload limits increased to 2 MB. This gives you more flexibility to use existing branding assets when customizing your web app. Previously, the console accepted only JPG and PNG files and limited logo uploads to 50 KB and favicon uploads to 20 KB.
