In November 2024, Amazon OpenSearch Service announced Extended Support for legacy Elasticsearch and OpenSearch engine versions (see also the detailed blog post ). Since that announcement, many customers have upgraded to newer versions. However, some customers need more time to plan and complete their migrations.
