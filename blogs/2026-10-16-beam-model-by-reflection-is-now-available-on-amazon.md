Beam, the first open weight model offered by US-based Reflection, is now available in Amazon SageMaker JumpStart, the model hub in Amazon SageMaker AI. According to Beam, they operate at the frontier of open weight models in its class, with strong performance across reasoning, coding, and agentic benchmarks. With it, customers can now build advanced coding agents and automated workflows on a frontier open weight model while keeping control of their data and infrastructure and adapting the model to their business.
