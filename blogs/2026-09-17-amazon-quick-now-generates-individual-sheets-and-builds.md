Amazon Quick now expands Generate Analysis with two new ways to create dashboards faster. You can generate a single sheet inside an existing analysis by describing it in natural language, and you can generate a new analysis from an image of an existing dashboard. With Generate Sheet, you describe the sheet you want and Amazon Quick adds it to your current analysis with visuals selected for your data, filter controls, and calculated fields such as year-over-year growth and month-over-month comparisons.
