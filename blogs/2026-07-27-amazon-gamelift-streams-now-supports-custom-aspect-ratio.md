Amazon GameLift Streams now supports Custom Aspect Ratio and Dynamic Resolution, giving you greater control over the streaming experience across diverse player devices and network conditions. With Custom Aspect Ratio, you can configure a specific resolution per stream session to match your player's device — including portrait, landscape, ultra-wide, and square aspect ratios. This eliminates letterboxing or pillarboxing, delivering native full-screen experiences on mobile phones, tablets, and non-standard displays.
