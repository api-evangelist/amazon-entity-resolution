Today, OpenAI announced that they are lowering API prices for GPT-5.6 Sol. Following the recent Terra and Luna price reductions, Sol now costs $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens—20% lower input pricing and 33.3% lower output pricing. This promotional pricing is available at least through November 21, 2026.
