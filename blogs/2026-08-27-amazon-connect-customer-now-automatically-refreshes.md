Amazon Connect Customer now automatically refreshes schedule metrics on the scheduling page, giving managers immediate visibility into the impact of schedule updates. For example, when a new team meeting is added for 10 agents from 10 AM – 11 AM, metrics such as net available headcount and projected service level are automatically updated. This gives workforce managers up-to-date visibility into how schedule changes affect staffing coverage, so they can make faster, more informed decisions throughout the day.
