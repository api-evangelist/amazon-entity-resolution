AWS Elastic Disaster Recovery (AWS DRS) now supports disaster recovery for AWS Graviton-based (arm64) source servers. You can now protect and recover your Graviton workloads with the same simple, reliable DRS experience you already use for x86 workloads. As more workloads move to Graviton for its price-performance benefits, they need the same disaster recovery coverage as the rest of your environment.
