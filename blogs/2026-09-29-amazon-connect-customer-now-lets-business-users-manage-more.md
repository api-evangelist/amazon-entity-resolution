Amazon Connect Customer now lets administrators store more of the reference data that drives their contact center configurations. Administrators can now use data tables to hold up to 20,000 values per table for defining configurations such as operating hours, escalation rules, AI agent prompts or queue assignments that drive contact flows. Administrators can now also export tables, edit hundreds of records, move tables between Connect Customer instances, or load reference data from external systems using tools and sources such as Excel and DynamoDB.
