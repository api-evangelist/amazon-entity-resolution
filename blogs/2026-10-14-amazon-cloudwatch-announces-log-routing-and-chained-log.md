Amazon CloudWatch now supports conditional log routing and chained parsers via pipelines, giving you more control over how log data is transformed and which CloudWatch log group it's sent to. CloudWatch pipelines is a fully managed service that ingests, transforms, and routes telemetry to CloudWatch without managing infrastructure. Log pipelines often carry a mix of event types that belong in different places, and individual events can nest one log format inside another.
