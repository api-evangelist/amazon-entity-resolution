Today, Amazon Quick adds support for building applications that use live data directly from your Quick datasets, so the KPIs, charts, tables, and insights in your application always reflect the latest data. These applications go beyond viewing data. They facilitate intelligent, data-driven business operations workflows where each person can act on what they see, from handling requests and approvals to triggering the next step.
