Today, AWS announces enhanced visualization for Amazon Q Developer in the AWS Management Console. Amazon Q now reasons across hundreds of AWS APIs to answer complex questions and deliver complete answers about your entire account—across all regions and services. Beyond the resource tables and cost charts introduced earlier this year, Amazon Q now renders health dashboards with status indicators, time-series charts with trend and anomaly detection, troubleshooting diagnostics, and resource detail views.
