AWS HealthOmics now supports sharing VPCs across accounts, enabling your workflows to access resources in Amazon Virtual Private Clouds (VPCs) managed by other AWS accounts with AWS Resource Access Manager (RAM). AWS HealthOmics is a HIPAA-eligible service that helps healthcare and life sciences customers accelerate scientific breakthroughs at scale with fully managed bioinformatics workflows.
