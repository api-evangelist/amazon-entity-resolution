Amazon Quick now offers Quick Max, a new plan for power users who want to get the absolute most out of Quick. With 5x the usage and 5x the storage of Plus, Max gives you the room to do more: more agents, more workflows, more of whatever makes Quick yours. With Max, you can run large, concurrent workloads without interruption—all month long.
