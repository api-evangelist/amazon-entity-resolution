Starting today, Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (Amazon EC2) G7e instances accelerated by NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs are now available in the AWS Europe (Frankfurt, Stockholm) and Asia Pacific (Mumbai) Regions. G7e instances offer up to 2.3x inference performance compared to G6e. Customers can use G7e instances to deploy large language models (LLMs), agentic AI models, multimodal generative AI models, and physical AI models.
