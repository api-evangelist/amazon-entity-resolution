Amazon Simple Email Service (SES) now supports open and click tracking override parameters in the SendEmail and SendBulkEmail APIs. Senders can enable or disable open tracking and click tracking on an individual API call, rather than managing tracking preferences through separate configuration sets. Previously, controlling tracking behavior required maintaining a distinct configuration set for each combination of open- and click-tracking settings.
