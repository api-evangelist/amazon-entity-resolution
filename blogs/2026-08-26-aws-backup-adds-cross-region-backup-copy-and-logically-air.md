AWS Backup now supports copying Amazon DocumentDB backups across AWS Regions and storing them in logically air-gapped vaults in nine additional AWS Regions: Asia Pacific (Hong Kong, Jakarta, Melbourne, Osaka), Europe (Spain, Stockholm, Zurich), Africa (Cape Town), and Israel (Tel Aviv). With cross-Region copy now available in these Regions, you can copy DocumentDB backups into and out of them using backup plans or on-demand copy jobs. This helps you meet disaster recovery, business continuity, and compliance requirements.
