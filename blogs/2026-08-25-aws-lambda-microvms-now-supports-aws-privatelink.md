AWS Lambda MicroVMs now supports AWS PrivateLink, enabling private connectivity to Lambda MicroVMs directly from Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) resources without exposing traffic to the public internet. This launch helps regulated workloads in financial services, healthcare, and government meet strict network isolation requirements when building with Lambda MicroVMs. PrivateLink VPC Endpoints deliver private connectivity to AWS services from your VPC, ensuring that your traffic does not travel over the public internet when communicating with AWS services.
