Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL 19 Beta 2 is now available in the Amazon RDS Database Preview Environment , allowing you to evaluate the pre-release of PostgreSQL 19 on Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL. You can deploy PostgreSQL 19 Beta 2 in the Amazon RDS Database Preview Environment that has the benefits of a fully managed database. PostgreSQL 19 introduces parallel autovacuum with configurable worker limits, so routine maintenance no longer bottlenecks large databases.
