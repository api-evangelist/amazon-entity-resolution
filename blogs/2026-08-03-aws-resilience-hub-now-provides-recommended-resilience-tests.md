AWS Resilience Hub now offers recommended resilience tests that help platform engineering and site reliability teams validate how their services respond to and recover from known failure scenarios. Resilience Hub provides pre-configured tests based on your service's architecture, configuration, and resilience policy. It uses AWS Fault Injection Service (FIS) to inject controlled faults and then evaluates whether your service recovers within your defined recovery objectives.
