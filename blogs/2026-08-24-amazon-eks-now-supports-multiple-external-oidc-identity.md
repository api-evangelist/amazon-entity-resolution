Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (Amazon EKS) now supports multiple external OpenID Connect (OIDC) identity providers per cluster. You can associate up to 10 OIDC identity providers with a single cluster, giving you more flexibility in how you authenticate users and workloads to your Kubernetes clusters. Many organizations use different identity providers for different user populations, such as employees, contractors, and CI/CD systems.
