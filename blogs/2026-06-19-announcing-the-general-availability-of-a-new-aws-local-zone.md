AWS introduces a new Local Zone in Hanoi, Vietnam, extending core infrastructure closer to end users. This deployment is among the first Asia Pacific Local Zones supporting Amazon S3 and Amazon EBS Local Snapshots, enabling customers to meet data residency requirements by storing and backing up data locally.
